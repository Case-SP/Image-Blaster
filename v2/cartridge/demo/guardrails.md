# Demo cartridge guardrails

- The output is always a single 4:5 image showing 6 small panels arranged 2 columns × 3 rows.
- Panels are equally sized squares with thin even white gutters between them.
- Background is uniform `#FAFAFA` warm off-white. No texture, no gradient, no color shift.
- No text, labels, numbers, captions, borders, or frames in the image.
- Each panel is photographic and self-contained — no panel bleeds into another.
