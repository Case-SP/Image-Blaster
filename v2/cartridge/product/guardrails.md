# Guardrails

- The object is the subject. Never substitute a similar object or "improve" it.
- Color stays honest — do not tint the object to match the backdrop or theme.
- Sketches are real ink-on-paper drawings, photographed flat. They are not 3D renders with a sketchy filter. The paper has visible grain; the ink has the texture of the chosen pen.
- In-situ shots are believable, not aspirational. The setting reads as a real place, not a styled set.
- No human figures unless the object only makes sense in a hand (e.g. a tool mid-use). Even then, hands and forearms only — never faces.
- No text on the object beyond what would naturally be there (a brand stamp, a serial number). Never add taglines, captions, or mock packaging copy.
